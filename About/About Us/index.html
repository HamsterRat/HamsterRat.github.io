About Helping Hands

Empowering our city, one resource at a time.

Welcome to Helping Hands!, your resouce hub for navigating the services in our community that keep us going while helping out our citizens. We believe that finding help, no matter the form, shouldn't be something difficult. Whether you are looking for options to donate to a food bank, healthcare support, or job opportunities, we've gathered it all here for you to brows and engage.

Our goal is to connect the citizens of Charlotte with the proper resources that will help them thrive and blossom into a connected population. By organizing local support from eager individuals and sorting the categories properly on our site, we will ensure that nobody within the city has to face adversity alone.

Our Core Values

  • Accessibility:Providing easy access to important information ensures that more people participate community uplift.
  • Community: Allowing the bond between these local organizations and the city's residents will build a stronger Charlotte.
  • Impact: By giving everyone the capabilities to give back and also host events, everyone can make an impact in Charlotte, big and small.
