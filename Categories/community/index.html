Habitat for Humanity Charlotte Region

This organization allows volunteers with any skill levels to help the city build affordable housing, aid with ReStore locations, or their participation in various youth and group programs. Prior to any of these events, training and guidance are provided before proceeding.

Mecklenburg County Parks & Recreation

Opportunities to maintain the city's recreational areas like parks and trails are offered here. If interested, various roles include picking up litter, caring for trails, and supporting facilities. This is an option best for those who are looking to give back to Mother Nature.

Volunteer with Parks & Rec →

TreesCharlotte

This organization relies on its volunteers to plant and care for trees along with packing seedlings and helping with outreach. Families, groups, and individuals are all able to make a visible impact on the city's urban forest and green spaces, bringing a more vibrant abundance of nature.

Volunteer with TreesCharlotte →

The Salvation Army of Greater Charlotte

Volunteers are given opportunities to serve meals, organize drives, help with various youth programs, and aid during seasonal programs. Anybody can get involved, to support those facing struggle in the community. Thorough instructions are given for all events available on the site to ensure success for all.
