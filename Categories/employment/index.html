CEOWorks Charlotte

This resource connects those who are seeking a job with the proper career development support. CEOWorks also provides job search assistance and work programs that allow people to build skills that lead to more secure and permanent employment.

Visit CEOWorks Charlotte →

CPCC – MORE Jobs NC

This is Central Piedmont Community College's program that allows invdividuals gain valuable skills that are highly valued in the current work force through training and education, creating futures of career advnacement and meaningful jobs that people are passionate about.

Learn More at CPCC →

Charlotte Mecklenburg Library – Job Help

The city's library provides free job search help, aid with creating and refining resumes, preparation for upcoming interviews, and access to other resources and technology that would support a Charlotte resident's journey into the workforce.

Visit Job Help at CMLibrary →

Classroom Central

This resource provides free school supplies for needy staff and students. By doing so, this ensure that all classrooms across the city are well equipped with the resources necessary to excel academically despite their socioeconomic status.

Visit Classroom Central →

Communities In Schools of Charlotte (CIS Charlotte)

This organization works within schools in the area to provide adequate academic support, mentorship, and other services that help remove any present barriers to student success and ultimately improving their attendance, behavior, and achievement of their goals.

Visit CIS Charlotte →
