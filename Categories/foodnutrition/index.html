Nourish Up

Nourish Up addresses food insecurity in Mecklenburg County through food pantries, grocery home delivery, and prepared meal programs. Formed from the merger of two long-standing nonprofits, it supports thousands of neighbors each year with access to nutritious food.

Visit Nourish Up →

Hope Street Food Pantry

Hope Street Food Pantry works to eradicate hunger in the city of Charlotte through food distribution. Their most remarkable program includes a mobile food pantry that partners with the Charlotte Mecklenburg Library. They focus on providing groceries full of nutrition to families suffering with food insecurity across the city. Hope Street also provides a variety of opportunities to volunteer and accepts monetary donations and food items to support their mission: “no barriers, no judgement, just support."

Visit Hope Street Food Pantry →

Feeding Charlotte

This organization deals with both hunger and food waste in the Charlotte area. By recovering fresh and excess food from restaurants, events, etc. and redistributing it to people who suffer from food insecurity, they ensure that there is enough in the community to feed everyone. Feeding Charlotte also works with volunteers to collect and redistribute food along with educating the public about the process of food recovery and hunger relief.

Visit Feeding Charlotte →

Second Harvest Food Bank of Metrolina

Second Harvest is a regional food bank that helps with over 20 counties in both Carolinas. They collect food donations and distribute them to a multitude of agencies like pantries and meal programs. Second Harvest also offers education and technical support. If you visit the site, there is information regarding these programs along with ways to help out the community through volunteering and donating.

Visit Second Harvest Metrolina →

Meals on Wheels North Carolina

Meals on Wheels North Carolina is a nonprofit organization that supports seniors and their providers with a combination of advocacy, training, and resource sharing. They focus on bolstering the amount of organizations that are able to deliver meals and support elders and other at risk populations across the state.

Visit Meals on Wheels NC →

What to Donate to a Food Bank

If you want to donate food to your local food bank, ensure that you choose the correct items that will help provide safe-to-consume meals for families in need. These institutions will typically accept most forms of nonperishable and shelf-stable items that can be easily stored.

Foods You Can Donate

  • Peanut butter
  • Canned liquids like stew and soup
  • Canned produce
  • Canned proteins like beans and tuna
  • Pasta
  • Rice
  • Liquids that do not require refrigeration, like cooking oil and dressings

Foods You Should Not Donate

  • Items that need refrigeration or freezing
  • Expired food
  • Leftovers or homemade food
  • Items with damaged packaging
  • Food packaged in unsafe materials like glass
