NAMI Charlotte

This organization supports people that are affected by mental health in the Mecklenburg area. They provide advocacy and education through various support groups and community programs. NAMI effectively helps families and suffering citizens navigate their journey through recovery with mental health resources, providing guidance and connection if necessary

Visit NAMI Charlotte →

Santé Group – Mecklenburg County

The Santé Group provides around the clock crisis support and behavioral help. Their Mobile Crisis Team is responsive to mental health emergencies and substance use troubles. Charlotte citizens are able to safely access the resources they need to recover.

Visit Santé Group Mecklenburg →

Mecklenburg County Emergency Contacts

This site gives a full list of local hotlines and crisis support numbers to contact. These include but are not limited to mental health, suicide prevention, domestic violence, along with other assistance lines in the Mecklenburg area to ensure the safety of its citizens

Mecklenburg County Health & Human Services (HHS)

Mecklenburg County HHS provides various resources for health, social services, and community support programs that include but are not limited to family support, senior services, and access to financial assistance.
