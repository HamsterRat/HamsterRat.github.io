Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new resource?

Click the "Add Resources" link in the navigation bar and fill out the form!

Is this service free to use?

Yes, Helping Hands is a 100% free community project for Charlotte residents.

Is my personal information safe when I sign up?

Absolutely. Helping Hands never sells your data to third parties. We only use it to keep you updated on community events. With the help of encryption, this is made possible.

Can I host an event through Helping Hands?

Yes! Navigate to "Make an Impact" and select "Host an Event." We provide the platform to help you recruit volunteers and spread the word to the Charlotte area.
